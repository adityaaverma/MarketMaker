# Market-Maker

Everything in `reference/` is Claude's first pass at this (Sep 12, 2026) --
a working Avellaneda-Stoikov engine in C++, with baseline experiments and
notes on what it found. Keep it around to check your own work against or
to peek at when genuinely stuck, but the point of this project is for you
to build it yourself.

Start fresh here at the top level: your own `include/`, `src/`, `Makefile`
(or CMake, your call), whatever structure you want. Ask Claude to explain
the Avellaneda-Stoikov math, review a diff, or help debug a specific error
-- not to write the implementation.
